# Feral Regions v2.0.0 — changed files

Upload every enclosed file directly into your existing FeralRegion repository root, replacing matches. sports.js and sports-ui.js are new required runtime modules. Do not upload the enclosing folder as a subfolder.

After Pages publishes, open the installed app online. Use Account → Save & load the app update when offered. Close and reopen if needed. Existing regions migrate; no reset or reinstall is needed.

REGIONAL SEASONS
Build a tournament arena. Seasons happen automatically, with seven game-days between completed championships by default. Tap the arena or open Tournaments for settings: interval 1–30 days, external prize pool $100–$10,000, paid places 1–16, and an automatic on/off switch.

Every trainer present when registration opens enters FREE, including the Elite Four. Team size may vary from 1–6. Current battles and starter selection finish first; everyone must have a reachable route to the arena. Existing custom events and an active Elite Four challenge finish before the season opens. Odd-sized brackets use byes. A lone trainer wins uncontested.

Seasons use healed copies of active teams: no real HP loss, XP rewards, or tonic consumption. Prizes come from an outside League fund, not the treasury. Higher placements receive more; trainers eliminated in the same round tie and share the allocation. This can include more people than the requested top-place cutoff. The full configured pool is distributed exactly once. Cancelled seasons pay no prizes and do not transfer their virtual pool to your treasury.

Season history retains champions and payouts; trophies, the season-title leaderboard and record book track wins. Elite Four residents return to their seats and original positions after release. Normal trainer quests, homes, and gold remain intact. New arrivals after the roster snapshot participate next season.

DUEL COURTS
Build → Buildings → Duel court ($950). Trainers no longer battle one another in wild catching areas. They travel to a court and queue for fights. These are ordinary battles with HP use, XP, and modest League prizes; trainers heal afterward. No trainer entry fee.

Place spectator stands within 14 tiles of a court or arena. Each stand belongs to its nearest venue and seats eight visitors. During duels the crowd grows and pays once per arriving visitor; spectators leave gradually when the court is quiet. Default tickets cost $2; change them to $0–$15 in court settings. Daily court upkeep is $4.

Tap a court for its queue, match count, audience, ticket revenue and Watch live button. You cannot move/remove it during a fight. Seasons temporarily release duel queues so everybody can join the championship. All event and queue state saves; the world still pauses while closed.
